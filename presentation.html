T-Test Findings & Interpretation

What is a T-Test? It compares the means (averages) of two groups (Stayers vs. Exiters) for continuous variables like Age and Tenure. It tells us if the difference in averages is statistically significant or just random noise.

Age at join (d ≈ -0.2)
Exiters are 1.5–2.4 years younger than stayers. Statistically significant but small effect size. Age modulates exit risk, but isn't the primary driver.
Tenure (d = -0.33 to -0.12)

Conclusion: Every test is significant (p<0.0001), but Cohen's d never exceeds 0.35. Attrition is structural and categorical, not solely driven by age or tenure profiles.

I-1
THE 30-DAY CLIFFOnboarding Velocity Score to replace generic induction
Chi² on Skill: V=0.446. <1-month exits rose 10.1% → 35.8% in 3 years.
Biggest driver of attrition growth is people leaving before finishing first month. Induction must be a moving score, not a calendar event.
Build real-time "Onboarding Velocity Score" (OVS) combining: attendance, first-pay flag, supervisor 1:1, skill mismatch. OVS < 60 triggers Day-12 chat.
Target: -20pp exit share
Owner: HR Ops
I-2
VENDOR ACCOUNTABILITYTiered SLA based on observed median tenure
Top 5 contractors = 46% of exits. Contractors AOT, IE, SSE lose people in weeks.
Extreme variance in retention between vendors. Contracts currently do not price for retention performance.
Renegotiate SLAs to "Tenure-Adjusted Rates": full rate only if 90 days completed. 50% rate for 30-day exit. Quarterly league table.
Target: 25% Reduction
Owner: Procurement
I-3
DEPARTMENT HEAT-ZONESFix local workflow in CHPRD + CHSTR
CHPRD + CHSTR = 66% of all attrition. Chi² on Dept V=0.57 in FY23-24.
When two departments produce 2/3rds of exits, the problem is local (shift patterns, workload) not the general market.
Commission 6-week diagnostic ONLY in CHPRD/CHSTR: ride-alongs, span-of-control audit, stay interviews with tenured staff.
Target: 10pp Drop
Owner: Plant HR
I-4
GENDER MIX LEVERQuadruple female representation
Female attrition 57.8% vs male 76.6% — a massive 19pp retention advantage.
Female contractors retain significantly better. Mix change alone has a leveraged effect on overall attrition rates.
Move from 2.3% → 10% female share in non-restricted roles (Quality, Stores, Ops). Pair with female-supervisor mentoring.
Target: 1.5pp Overall
Owner: CHRO
I-5
PREDICTIVE STAY-SCOREDeployment screening and mentoring
Male Semi-Skilled Helper <21 at CHPRD via AOT has >90% exit probability.
Attrition is the joint outcome of a specific profile. The data can score this risk at the point of hiring.
Build logistic-regression "Stay Score". Candidates in high-risk bands must be paired with senior mentors or re-routed to lower-attrition depts.
Target: 10pp Reduction
Owner: Analytics
I-6
SEASONAL DEFENSEPre-empt Jan-Feb-May-Sep exit waves
Jan, Feb, May, Sep hold ~60% of annual exits combined.
Exits cluster around festive cycles and migration. These waves are predictable and can be mitigated.
Run "Stay-bonus campaigns" 4 weeks BEFORE each peak. Completion bonuses paid in next salary cycle for those present during peak.
Target: 1,500 Retained
Owner: C&B
I-7
DATA VISIBILITYBoard-level median tenure tracking
Exit count rose 65% over 3 FYs, but <1-month exit share rose 254%.
Static headcount/exit counts hide the velocity of deterioration. Tenure metrics provide better early warning.
Add to monthly HR dashboard: Median tenure of exiters, % of exits within 30 days, Contractor Tenure-Adjusted Cost per Hire.
Enables Tracking
Owner: COO
